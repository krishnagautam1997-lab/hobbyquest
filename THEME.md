# HobbyQuest — Crimson Curiosity

The founder-selected reference establishes ink-black surfaces, hot-red neon, brush headlines, cinematic imagery, and streetwear details. It replaces the earlier cyan/violet Neon Atlas direction.

## Layout and imagery

One spacious vertical homepage: hero, editorial spotlight, discoveries, saved worlds, and unexpected rabbit holes. Topic collections scroll horizontally with touch, trackpad, keyboard, or visible arrows. The spotlight is curated rather than a claim about live popularity. Unknown topics receive a clearly decorative cover rather than a misleading photograph.

Background #050708; surface #0b0f12; text #f7f6f5; accent #ff174b; borders #25292e. Typography uses clean Inter headings and reading text with local sans-serif fallbacks; Permanent Marker is reserved for the expressive hero and accent stamps. Larger labels, descriptions, and controls replace the earlier tiny/condensed treatment. Original generated imagery is stored in assets/.

## Motion

Slow crimson/violet ambient light drifts across the entire page, including touch layouts. Soft crimson cursor illumination, nearby particle connections, gentle card response, and subtle hero parallax remain desktop-only. Only transforms animate the ambient layers; no video, extra images, or new service is needed. Keep native cursors and opaque reading dialogs; dim and pause ambient movement while dialogs are open. Respect reduced motion, offer a saved motion switch on desktop and touch, and pause background-tab animation.

## Real activity

Count unique worlds opened, currently saved interests, and discoveries explicitly collected. Optional First Spark, World Hopper, and Collector badges recognise actual local actions. No learned percentages, pressure streaks, or compulsory quizzes.

## Boundaries

Sign in opens an honest account preview. No credentials, registration, or cross-device sync are connected. Guest records stay in this browser. Existing IDs and notes are preserved when custom interests gain curated guides or covers. Topic dialogs contain labelled example guides and separate notes. Formula 1 now has the first full scrolling topic-world page, with eight source-linked prepared stories, story saves, and separate F1 notes. Other full worlds, secure accounts, photo/voice input, live image lookup, and live AI remain future work.

## Round Earth and first world — 17 October

Founder refinement: Space Grotesk adds an editorial voice to headings and small labels, with larger body text, stronger category accents, and slightly wider cards. Inter stays for long-form reading; the brush hero stays expressive. Scroll-linked child surfaces zoom gently into place on entry and away on exit, while hero copy eases back. Layout parents are never transformed, so sticky navigation and scroll geometry remain stable. No scroll hijacking or hidden-until-JavaScript content; Motion off and reduced motion disable the effect. Notes and story reading stay calm.

The homepage hero now uses a round red Earth instead of the static portrait/planet composite. Its map is spherically projected with a small local WebGL shader, so continents curve and rotate around the globe rather than spinning as a flat disc. A CSS sphere is the fallback. It remains visibly round on mobile, above the hero copy. Respect the saved motion preference and system reduced motion; stop rendering when offscreen, in hidden tabs, and behind dialogs. Cap drawing resolution and avoid a dependency or video player.

The Watches cover is original illustrative Patek Philippe Nautilus artwork, not an official photograph or endorsement. The broad Watches topic remains intact.
