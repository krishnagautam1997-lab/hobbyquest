## Round Earth, Patek cover, and F1 world — 17 October 2026

Created using the built-in image-generation tool. Saved project assets: assets/earth-red.webp, assets/patek-nautilus.webp, assets/f1-hero.webp, assets/f1-stories.webp. Compression preserves the complete original compositions. The Earth image is a surface texture, not the visible shape; globe.js wraps it onto a round, shaded sphere. Geographic imagery is decorative, not a map for navigation. Patek Philippe and Formula 1 are not affiliated with this prototype. The watch is an illustrative depiction, not an official product photograph; racing scenes do not depict the historical events described.

The Nautilus design reference is [Patek Philippe’s 2006 press material](https://static.patek.com/pdf/pressreleases/en/2006_PatekPhilippe_Nautilus_2006.pdf). No third-party product/event photos were copied into the repository.

### Generation prompts

#### assets/earth-red.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless equirectangular Earth surface texture for a web globe, flat rectangular 2:1 map 2048x1024. Create a geographically recognisable world map texture, flat equirectangular projection, covering all longitudes and latitudes: Americas on left, Europe and Africa near middle, Asia and Australia on right, Antarctica along bottom, Greenland upper left-middle. Ocean very dark almost black burgundy; land luminous rich crimson red with subtle terrain detail; very fine red city-like specks, restrained and modern, not busy. This is a flat surface texture that will be wrapped on a 3D sphere, NOT a sphere image. Illumination EVEN across the whole flat map, no directional shading, vignette, glow around the edges, star field, text, labels, grid lines or border. Opposite left and right edges should match seamlessly with dark ocean. Cinematic premium red Earth look. Land silhouettes recognisable, no fantasy continents. No typography, no UI.

#### assets/patek-nautilus.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: portrait 3:4 topic-card illustration for HobbyQuest. Subject: an illustrative Patek Philippe Nautilus 5711 in steel with characteristic rounded octagonal brushed bezel and side ears, integrated polished/brushed steel bracelet, dark blue horizontally embossed dial, baton hour markers, simple date aperture at three, three central hands, no chronograph subdials. Small dial branding may read PATEK PHILIPPE and GENEVE; no other overlay text. One watch only, face large and readable centred in the upper two-thirds, bracelet leading diagonally to the lower right. Premium realistic editorial macro rendering, sophisticated black background, subtle crimson neon rim light and a little cool blue reflection, fine metal textures. Keep the bottom 15 percent dark for HTML copy. This is original illustrative product artwork, not an official advertisement or authorised product photograph. No box, people, hands, extra watches, watermark or price.

#### assets/f1-hero.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide 16:9 cinematic hero background for HobbyQuest Formula 1 topic-world page, landscape 1792x1024. One original unbranded red open-wheel racing car photographed from a low three-quarter rear-side angle, car fills right two-thirds of the composition with rear tyre and bodywork beautifully detailed. Night racing circuit, crimson neon reflections on wet black asphalt, restrained electric blue accents in distance, realistic premium cinematic art rather than cartoon. Keep the entire left third very dark and low-detail for large HTML title and introductory text. Red speed-light streaks and subtle atmospheric haze, but car crisp. Strong sculptural silhouette, sophisticated futuristic racing vibe consistent with a black/red streetwear editorial site. This is an illustrative racing scene, not a real event, not an exact current-season vehicle. No driver face, brand logos, sponsor marks, letters, text, UI, watermark, borders or extra cars.

#### assets/f1-stories.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single production sprite sheet of eight F1 explainer-story cover illustrations for HobbyQuest. One gapless 4-column, 2-row grid, overall landscape 2048x768, each of the 8 equal cells is a landscape 4:3 image with no border or gutter. Top row left to right: 1 close-up original racing front wing with fine red airflow visualisation; 2 an anonymous pit crew changing an open-wheel race car tyre; 3 two original open-wheel racing cars competing side by side at a floodlit corner; 4 two anonymous racing helmets beside one another on a dark garage shelf. Bottom row left to right: 5 anonymous race engineer wearing a headset in a control garage with soft screens but no readable text; 6 driver gloved hands on a racing steering wheel seen from cockpit, no face; 7 a vintage 1950s-style unbranded open-wheel racing car on a quiet English circuit, no real archival photo claim; 8 a modern racing-car body detail shown alongside an older sculptural racing-car nose, a visual conversation across design eras. Premium cinematic photoreal editorial look, ink-black backgrounds and hot crimson lighting with restrained cool blue accents, consistent visual palette, grounded materials, original illustrative racing scenes not real events. Keep each subject completely within its own cell, comfortably inset for minor cropping. Absolutely no lettering, text, logos, sponsor marks, UI, captions, watermarks, borders or cell dividers.
