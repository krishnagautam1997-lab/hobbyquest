No stock-image subscription or paid asset purchase was used. Original compositions are preserved and compressed to WebP. CSS selects individual cells of the photographic sheets. ## Expanded worlds artwork

Saved asset: `assets/culture-worlds.webp`. Created with the built-in image-generation tool, not an API-key workflow. Compressed without changing the complete composition.

Generation prompt: One gapless 4-column by 2-row sheet of eight equal portrait panels. Top row: unbranded premium sneaker; anonymous adult K-pop performance with lightsticks; original anime adult traveller above a neon city, no existing franchise; ancient stone columns and historical bust. Bottom row: cricket bat, red ball and stumps at a floodlit ground; anonymous adult model in modern black tailoring; unbranded perfume bottle; unbranded midnight sports car on wet asphalt. Premium cinematic black/crimson palette with violet, blue and amber accents, realistic editorial texture except the anime panel, subjects centred in their own cells, dark lower area for HTML titles. No text, logos, watermarks, UI, gutters or borders. Illustrative artwork, not real-event photography.
